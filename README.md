# Coffee Sales Data Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using Excel)

## Project Objective
The coffee company wants to understand its sales performance across countries, customers, and product types from 2019 to 2022. So that the business can identify its strongest markets and customers, and grow more sales going forward.

## Dataset used
- [Coffee Orders Dataset](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1XSW71_uDr5MIIO2niFSk06biV6Ft-T-i/edit?usp=drivesdk&ouid=116314777286714285295&rtpof=true&sd=true)

## Questions (KPIs)
- How have total sales trended over time (2019-2022)?
- Which coffee type - Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, or Robusta - sells the most?
- Which country generates the highest sales?
- Who are the top 5 customers by sales value?
- How do sales vary by roast type - Dark, Light, or Medium?
- How do sales vary by package size - 0.2kg, 0.5kg, 1.0kg, or 2.5kg?
- What impact does having a loyalty (Royalty) card have on customer purchases?
- Dashboard Interaction [View Dashboard]

## Process
- Verified data for any missing values and anomalies across the orders, customers, and products sheets, and sorted out the same.
- Made sure data was consistent and clean with respect to data type, data format, and values used.
- Built pivot tables according to the questions asked.
- Merged all pivot tables into one dashboard and applied slicers (Order Date, Roast Type, Size, Royalty Card) to make it dynamic.

## Project Insight
- The United States is by far the largest market, generating $34,281 in sales, well ahead of Ireland ($6,440) and the United Kingdom ($2,593).
- Allis Wilmore is the top customer at $317, followed closely by Brenn Dundredge ($307), Terri Farra ($289), Nealson Cuttler ($282), and Don Flintiff ($278).
- Total sales fluctuate month to month with no single dominant coffee type, though Arabica and Excelsa show the highest recurring peaks across the 2019-2022 period.
- Sales data spans from December 2019 through September 2022, giving a near three-year view of purchasing patterns.
- The dashboard allows filtering by roast type, package size, and loyalty card status, enabling deeper segmentation of the customer base.

## Final Conclusion:
To grow coffee sales, the business should focus on strengthening its position in the United States, its strongest market by a wide margin, while investing in targeted campaigns to grow the smaller Ireland and UK markets. Nurturing relationships with top customers like Allis Wilmore and Brenn Dundredge, along with promoting loyalty card sign-ups, could help convert repeat buyers into a more predictable revenue base.
